{{ t("Why we check your details now") }}

Most delayed procurement payments are not caused by the procurement itself. They are caused by an Aadhaar digit typed wrong, an IFSC that does not exist, or a bank account in a relative's name.

Those mismatches are normally discovered weeks after the produce is sold. This portal checks them the moment you register, so there is time to fix them before your slot.
