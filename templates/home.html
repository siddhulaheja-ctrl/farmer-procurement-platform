What this portal does differently

Detail mismatches are caught at registration

Aadhaar, bank account, IFSC and land-record details are checked the moment they are entered. In existing systems the mismatch surfaces weeks later, when the payment fails. Here the farmer and the centre both see it before the slot date.

{{ t("Storage risk") }} is flagged before the grain spoils

If a slot is more than five days away and rain or high humidity is forecast for the farmer's district, the portal warns them and offers an earlier slot — instead of letting the produce sit and get downgraded at the gate.
